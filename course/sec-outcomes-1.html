    Subsection R1 From Derivative Graph to Function Graph

    R1.

    I can interpret the graph of a rate of change or the derivative \(f'(x)\) to describe and construct the graph of the function \(f(x)\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 1.

    The following graph is the graph of \(y=f'(x)\text{,}\) where \(f'(x)\) is the derivative of another function \(f(x)\text{.}\) Interpret \(f'(x)\) in order to draw a potential graph of \(y=f(x)\text{.}\) Your graph must clearly demonstrate correct intervals for increasing, decreasing, and concavity. Label the x-values for all local extremes and inflection points.
    🔗

    Subsection R2 Definite Integrals for Net Change in Quantity

    R2.

    I can calculate the definite integral of a rate function using geometric area formulas and interpret the value as the total increment of the changing value.
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 3.

    The graph below represents the rate \(r(t)\) at which water is being added to a tank (or removed, as appropriate) in gallons/minute. If the tank started (\(t=0\)) with 15 gallons, state and use an appropriate definite integral to find the amount of water at \(t=10\) minutes. A correct solution must show the correct notation for the definite integral and calculate its value using appropriate geometric formulas.
    🔗

    Subsection R3 Applying Properties of Definite Integrals

    R3.

    I can apply a combination of the properties of definite integrals, including the splitting property, the sum rule, and the constant multiple rule, to compute the value of a definite integral involving a combination of two functions.
    🔗
    🔗

    Checkpoint 5.

    Suppose that for a function \(f(x)\) (not shown) we have \(\displaystyle \int_1^3 f(x) \, dx = 5\) and \(\displaystyle \int_1^8 f(x) \,dx = 2\text{.}\) A graph of \(g(x)\) is shown below with regions between the graph and the \(x\)-axis labeled with areas \(R_1=4\text{,}\) \(R_2=3\) and \(R_3=6\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Use this information to find \(\displaystyle \int_3^8 2f(x) + g(x) \, dx\text{.}\)
