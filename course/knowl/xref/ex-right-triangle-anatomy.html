Worksheet Exercise 1. The Anatomy of a Right Triangle.

A right triangle has one angle of \(90^\circ\text{,}\) called the right angle. The two sides that meet at the right angle are the legs. The third side, opposite the right angle, is the hypotenuse. The other two angles are less than \(90^\circ\) and are called acute angles.
🔗
Throughout this activity we draw the right angle on the right side of the triangle, at the vertex \(C\text{.}\) The angle at \(B\) is \(\theta\text{,}\) the angle at \(A\) is \(\phi\text{,}\) and the side lengths are labeled \(a\text{,}\) \(b\text{,}\) and \(c\) as shown.
🔗

(a)

Suppose \(\theta=35^\circ\text{.}\) Find \(\phi\text{.}\) Then explain why, in every right triangle, the two acute angles satisfy \(\theta+\phi=90^\circ\text{.}\) (Two angles that add to \(90^\circ\) are called complementary.)
