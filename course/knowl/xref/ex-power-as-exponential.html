Worksheet Exercise 4. Every Power Is a Natural Exponential.

By the definition of real powers, \(\ln(u^w)=w\ln(u)\) for \(u\gt 0\text{.}\) Reading this as an exponential equation gives
\begin{equation*} u^w = e^{w\ln(u)} \qquad (u\gt 0)\text{.} \end{equation*}
This lets us treat any power, including ones where both the base and the exponent vary, as a natural exponential.
